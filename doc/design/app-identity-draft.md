# Application identity — first directions

Exploratory names and original vector marks, not an accepted product identity.
[Open the comparison sheet](app-identity-draft.svg). Names and marks can be mixed.
No executable, application ID, settings namespace or installer has been renamed.

* **Svit:** the first preference. Short, ASCII, and related to light in Czech. The mark
  combines two open framing corners and a small warm disc, suggesting a picture seen
  in a frame. It gives the application an identity that also works beyond photographs.
* **Osvit:** a photographic naming direction. The mark uses overlapping picture planes;
  this is more literal about browsing and light editing. Its small mountain detail
  needs simplification in a 16 px version.
* **Pruzor:** an ASCII rendering of “průzor”, a viewing aperture/window. The P and open
  corner are a more geometric utility direction. This needs an international
  pronunciation check before choosing it for a broad audience.

The palette is deliberately fixed: slate #27343e, mint #81d8c5, amber #f5bd66 and
warm white #f3f3ec. It should identify the application regardless of the chosen UI hue.
Marks must remain legible on light and dark desktops, in monochrome, and at small sizes.
These drafts use ordinary scaling; a selected design needs separate optical adjustments
for 16/24/32 px, then SVG/PNG/ICO packaging after approval.

A preliminary web search found existing photo/media software named
[Glint](https://helloglint.com/), [Kestrel](https://www.projectkestrel.org/), and
[Framelet](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/framelet-memory-slideshow/id6796186910),
so those are excluded from this first shortlist. This is an initial collision check,
not a claim that the proposed names are cleared or available everywhere.

Word references: [svit — Seznam](https://slovnik.seznam.cz/preklad/cesky_anglicky/svit),
[průzor — Lingea](https://slovniky.lingea.cz/anglicko-cesky/pr%C5%AFzor).
